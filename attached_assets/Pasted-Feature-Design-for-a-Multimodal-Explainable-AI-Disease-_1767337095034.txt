🔹 FEATURE DESIGN REQUIREMENTS (MANDATORY)
1️⃣ Core Risk Prediction

Define features for:

Disease risk probability (%)

Risk categories (Low / Medium / High)

Confidence indicator

Explain:

Why these outputs matter clinically

How doctors interpret them

2️⃣ Multimodal Patient Data Intake

Design features for accepting:

Tabular data (vitals, labs)

Time-series data (heart rate / ECG)

Medical images (X-ray placeholder)

Focus on:

UX clarity

Clinical realism

Logical grouping of inputs

3️⃣ Explainability & Trust

Design features for:

Patient-level explanation (local)

Population-level explanation (global)

Feature contribution ranking

Explain how:

A clinician would interpret explanations

Trust is built without oversimplification

4️⃣ Counterfactual & “What-If” Analysis

Design features that:

Allow users to simulate changes (BP, glucose, BMI)

Enforce clinically realistic constraints

Show impact on risk

Avoid:

Unrealistic instant changes

Prescription-like suggestions

5️⃣ Uncertainty & Reliability

Design features for:

Prediction confidence levels

Uncertainty warnings

Data quality alerts

Explain:

When the system should not be trusted fully

6️⃣ Bias, Fairness & Ethics

Design features for:

Bias reporting by age and gender

Fairness alerts

Ethical transparency

Include:

What the system should say when bias is detected

7️⃣ Clinical Workflow Support

Design features for:

Patient risk history timeline

Risk trend visualization

Comparison of past vs current risk

Focus on:

Long-term disease monitoring

8️⃣ Safety & Legal Guardrails

Design features that ensure:

Clear medical disclaimers

No deterministic or diagnostic language

AI is positioned as decision support only

Explicitly define:

What the system must NEVER claim
